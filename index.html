Privacy Policy

Official Data Protection & Privacy Governance for Nine Star Technologies

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

Nine Star Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting user privacy across all our published mobile applications, websites, and affiliated digital software products. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is safeguarded, and how you can exercise your legal rights regarding your data.

2. Scope & Applicable Applications

This policy applies to all applications published by Nine Star Technologies on the Google Play Store and other authorized platforms. Each specific app might feature localized supplementary disclosures depending on distinct device permissions requested.

3. Information We Collect

  • Information You Provide Voluntarily: When you register an account, contact customer service, or submit inquiries, we may process your email address, name, and communication logs.
  • Device and Telemetry Data: We may collect non-personal device identifiers, operating system version, app version, language settings, and anonymous crash analytics (e.g., via Google Play Services) to resolve bugs and optimize responsiveness.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not collect health data, financial account credentials, or exact real-time GPS locations unless an individual application specifically and explicitly requests your opt-in consent for a core feature.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

We process information pursuant to legitimate business interests (troubleshooting, continuous product optimization), performance of contracts (enabling requested application capabilities), and user consent where legally mandated by regional privacy frameworks including GDPR and CCPA.

5. Third-Party Services & Libraries

Our applications may interface with trusted service providers strictly to perform core operational functions:

  • Google Play Services (Authentication, in-app purchases, crash telemetry)
  • Supabase (Encrypted database management and secure synchronization)

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Requests

We retain data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or comply with regulatory mandates. Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated telemetry data at any time by emailing support@ninestartechnologies.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Requests are handled within 30 business days.

7. Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit and AES-256 for resting data) to prevent unauthorized interception, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or regulatory concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our privacy compliance officer at:

Nine Star Technologies
Email: support@ninestartechnologies.com
