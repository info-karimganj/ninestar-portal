Privacy policy

Official Data Protection & Privacy Governance for Nine Star Technologies. Effective Date: January 1, 2025.

Information we collect

Our applications may process basic telemetry, anonymous crash analytics, and device specifications (OS version, language) strictly to ensure stability. No sensitive financial, biometric, or continuous GPS telemetry is harvested without explicit user consent.
